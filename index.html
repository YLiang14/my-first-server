My favorite holiday traditions!

Chinese New Year

Family and relatives get together to eating some traditional chinese food.

The type of food we have is roast pork, whole chicken, fish and shrimp.

Parents and older relatives give you red envelopes filled with money.

You get wear something new is can bring you good luck for the new year ahead.

You can play with fireworks! and you go to Chinatown which you can see the parade with lion dance.

Dessert you get to is sweet rice balls!
